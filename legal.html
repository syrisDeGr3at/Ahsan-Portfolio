Privacy

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

It saves one item in your browser’s local storage, ahsan-mode, to remember your light or dark choice, and a short-lived session flag, ahsan-arrive, that plays the door transition when you open a project.

The contact form doesn’t send anything to a server. It writes a message for you, copies it, and opens LinkedIn so you can send it yourself.

The working apps (Taskflow, Ledger, Textcraft) keep their data in your own browser only.
